# Parking Ticket Fighter, Stage 2: User Stories
Drafted October 12, 2026. Persona approved by Risha October 12, 2026. Awaiting Risha's approval before Stage 3.

## Must have

1. As Denise, I want to photograph my ticket and have the app read the ticket number, date, time, zone, and fine amount, so that I do not have to type government form details by hand.
2. As Denise, I want the app to ask me one question first, "Did you pay for parking when this ticket was written," so that my case starts down the right path.
3. As Denise, I want a plain language case check that tells me if my ticket is winnable and why, so that I do not waste money contesting a lost cause or walk away from a winning one.
4. As Denise, I want a guided evidence checklist (ticket photo, parking app payment screenshot, bank or card charge, receipts), so that I gather exactly the proof that wins and nothing I do not need.
5. As Denise, I want the app to build my contest packet as one clean PDF, ticket photo plus evidence plus a written statement of what happened, so that I can submit it as is.
6. As Denise, I want step by step submission instructions for my city (online portal, mail, or in person, with the address and what to write where), so that I file it correctly the first time.
7. As Denise, I want the app to calculate and show my contest deadline and the discount window deadline ($22.50 before it becomes $45), so that I never lose money to a date printed in fine print.
8. As Denise, I want reminders before those deadlines and after I file, while I wait for the result letter, so that the ticket never quietly turns into a bigger fine or a boot risk.
9. As Denise, I want to track my filing status (not filed, filed and waiting, dismissed, upheld), so that I always know where my ticket stands.
10. As Denise, I want to pay $19 per ticket inside the app only when I download or submit my packet, so that I only pay when the app has actually done the work.

## Should have

11. As Denise, I want the app to spot duplicate parking charges on my card statement (the same session charged twice by the parking app), so that I can dispute those too.
12. As Denise, I want to save my vehicles and payment methods, so that my second ticket takes half the time of my first.
13. As Denise, I want the app to tell me honestly when paying the ticket is cheaper than contesting it, so that I trust its advice when it says fight.
14. As Marcus, I want my drivers to upload a ticket photo the day it is issued from their own phones, so that no ticket reaches me weeks late.
15. As Marcus, I want one dashboard showing every vehicle, every ticket, every deadline, and every status, so that nothing expires unchallenged by accident.
16. As Marcus, I want packets built in bulk for the winnable tickets, so that contesting a month of tickets takes minutes, not an office day.

## Nice to have

17. As Denise, I want a map of my past tickets and zones, so that I can see which blocks keep costing me money.
18. As Denise, I want a warning when I park in a zone where I have been ticketed before, so that the app prevents the next ticket, not just fights it.
19. As Marcus, I want a monthly report of fines paid, fines dismissed, and money saved, so that I can see the plan is worth it.
20. As Denise, I want the same flow adapted for other cities beyond Detroit, so that the app travels with me when I park in Chicago or Cleveland.

## Detroit specifics baked into these stories (from Risha's real cases)
- Administrative Review is the contest path; submissions go through parkdetroit.us, by mail to the Parking Violations Bureau, P.O. Box 2549, Detroit, MI 48231-2549, or in person at 1001 Tenth Street, Detroit, MI 48216. Phone: (313) 963-9630.
- Filing freezes penalties while the review is pending; results arrive by US mail within about 30 days.
- Fine structure seen in her cases: Code 821 at $22.50 inside the window, $45 after, with late fees possible on unresolved tickets.
- Winning evidence in her strongest case was a paid session receipt matching the ticket's zone, space, and time window.
