# Parking Ticket Fighter, Full AI Pass
Chosen by Risha October 13, 2026: "Full AI: reader, coach, statement writer, defense finder."
Builds on the Stage 6 working app and the OCR fix pass (front and back reading, in browser OCR fallback, metadata stripping).

## The four AI features
1. **AI Reader (vision).** The server vision reading of front and back ticket photos stays the primary reader. It extracts fields, transcribes full text for both sides, and cross checks front versus back, flagging mismatches for the user to resolve on Confirm Details. In browser OCR remains the automatic fallback when the AI reader is unavailable or unreadable. The user confirms or corrects every field. Nothing AI read is treated as final until the user confirms it.
2. **AI Case Coach.** After the one question and evidence entry, an AI review of the ticket record (answers, evidence present or missing, session time and zone match, deadlines) produces: the verdict (Strong, Worth filing, Weak), a plain language explanation tied to the actual facts in the record, the specific evidence gap that would most improve the case, and the honest money math (pay $22.50 by the discount date versus risk $45 plus late fees). The rule based verdict stays visible if AI is unavailable, labeled as the standard check. AI must not promise a dismissal or a win rate.
3. **AI Statement Writer.** Drafts the contest statement from the confirmed facts only (ticket, zone, session window, evidence list, defense found). Formal, clear, first person. The user edits freely before it enters the packet. The statement screen keeps the warning: sign only what is true. If a fact is missing, the draft leaves a clear bracketed prompt for the user, it never fills the gap with an invented detail.
4. **AI Defense Finder.** For the did not pay path and for weak cases, AI asks follow up questions one at a time from the approved defense list (meter broken, sign missing or wrong, loading, medical emergency, wrong plate or vehicle, paid session ending just after ticket time, app payment failure) plus anything the ticket photos and record suggest (for example a zone or time inconsistency between the ticket and the session proof). A found defense attaches its evidence item to the checklist and re runs the case coach. If no defense is found, the honest weak case screen still shows, unchanged in spirit.

## Guardrails, non negotiable
- AI never invents a fact, a date, a payment, or a city rule. It works only from the ticket record, the photos read, and the user's answers. Unknowns are stated as unknowns.
- Every AI output is labeled as AI help inside the app, with the line that this is guidance, not legal advice.
- The user confirms all AI read fields and can edit all AI written text. AI output never files, submits, or sends anything.
- Detroit facts in the app (Administrative Review, parkdetroit.us, P.O. Box 2549, 1001 Tenth Street, (313) 963-9630, penalties frozen during review, results by mail in about 30 days) come from the verified Stage 2 record, not from the AI model. If AI text conflicts with those, the verified facts win and the AI text is not shown.
- Privacy: photos sent to the AI reader use the stripped, generic named copies from the OCR pass. App copy states plainly that ticket details are sent to an AI service for reading and case help, and are stored with the ticket record in the app.
- No em dashes in any app copy, including AI prompt output instructions.

### What was built
- Reader: unchanged from the OCR passes (server AI vision primary, in browser Tesseract fallback, generic name redraw). Photo slot success copy now labels the AI reader and reminds the user the read fields are a draft to check.
- AI Case Coach: server action `aiCoach`. Sends only the record (paid answer, evidence with session start, end, and zone, deadlines, attached defenses) to the AI with a no invention, no promise, no filing facts prompt. Stores verdict, explanation, top gap, and server computed money math on the ticket (migration 0004). Shown in a labeled AI Case Coach panel in Case check, with the standard check still visible above it. Guardrails in code: em dashes stripped, and AI text naming a conflicting P.O. Box, phone, or street address is rejected and replaced by the standard check, labeled as such.
- AI Statement Writer: server action `aiWriteStatement`. Drafts from confirmed facts only, bracketed prompts for anything missing, result lands in the existing editable statement box with the sign only what is true warning. Standard template fallback (record facts only) if AI is unavailable, labeled standard.
- AI Defense Finder: server action `aiFindDefenses` plus `applyDefense`. Deterministic candidates from answers and record inconsistencies (zone mismatch, session edge timing), AI summary, extra allowed defenses, and up to 3 follow up questions shown one at a time in the client. Applying a defense attaches its evidence item (type defense) and recomputes the verdict. Verdict logic refined in this pass: a paid case whose session matches but whose proof set is incomplete is now Worth filing instead of Strong, so the coach test (b) expectation holds in both the AI and standard paths.
- Copy: Honest limits and both privacy notes now describe the AI features, the labeling, the guidance not legal advice line, that verified Detroit facts override AI text, and that ticket details are sent to an AI service for reading and case help.
