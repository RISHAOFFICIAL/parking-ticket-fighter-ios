# Parking Ticket Fighter, Stage 1: User Persona
Drafted October 12, 2026. Awaiting Risha's approval before Stage 2.

## Primary persona: Denise, the driver who paid and still got ticketed

- **Who:** Denise Carter, 41, home health aide living in Detroit. Drives daily for work, parks at meters downtown and in Midtown several times a week, and pays through the Park Detroit app.
- **Her situation:** She got a $45 parking ticket even though she paid for her session in the app. The app glitched during payment, and she now has screenshots, a bank charge, and a ticket that says she did not pay. She has never contested a ticket before and does not know where to start.
- **Goals:**
  - Get the ticket dismissed without taking a day off work or standing in line.
  - Know exactly what evidence she needs and whether her case is strong before she files.
  - Never miss the contest deadline, because the fine goes up and late fees stack on.
- **Frustrations:**
  - The city's process is scattered across a website, a phone line, a P.O. box, and an in person office, and none of it explains what proof actually wins.
  - She has the proof (app screenshots, a card charge) but does not know how to organize it into a filing.
  - Deadlines are printed in fine print, and the discount window closes fast ($22.50 becomes $45, then late fees on top).
- **Tech comfort:** Confident on her phone, takes screenshots, uses Cash App and banking apps. Not comfortable writing formal letters or figuring out government forms.
- **What success looks like:** She photographs the ticket, the app walks her through gathering her payment proof, builds her contest packet as a clean PDF, tells her exactly where and by when to submit it, and reminds her until the result letter arrives. Total time for her: about 10 minutes.
- **What she will pay:** $19 per ticket feels fair if the app saves her a $45 fine, a late fee, and a trip downtown. She would pay a small monthly plan if she parked downtown every day.

## Secondary persona: Marcus, the small fleet owner

- **Who:** Marcus Reed, 52, runs a 6 van delivery and service company out of Detroit. His drivers collect several tickets a month across the city.
- **His situation:** Tickets land on his desk weeks later, some already past the discount window. He cannot tell which ones are worth contesting.
- **Goals:** One place where drivers upload tickets the day they get them, deadlines tracked automatically, and contest packets built in bulk for the winnable ones.
- **What success looks like:** No ticket ever expires unchallenged by accident, and contesting costs him minutes instead of an office day.
- **What he will pay:** A monthly plan per vehicle makes sense if it saves him even two fines a month.

## Built from real life
This persona is drawn directly from Risha's own October 2026 experience: three Detroit tickets in one week (Y18973750, Y18981274, Y18776021), two of them issued during paid Park Detroit app sessions, app payment errors, duplicate pending card charges, evidence packets built by hand, Administrative Review submissions on parkdetroit.us, results by mail within about 30 days, and hard contest deadlines with a $22.50 to $45 step up plus late fee risk. The app exists so the next Denise does in 10 minutes what took days of back and forth.
